## 1. Retention-Optimized Script (~166 words)

### HOOK (0:00–0:07)
> Three lines from the Bhagavad Gita will **completely change** how you work.
> **Action. Mind. Path.**
> Let's break them down.

### POINT 1: Action (0:07–0:24)
> Lesson one: do the work… **drop the result.**
> You control your effort. You will *never* control the outcome.
> Champions train the process, not the trophy.
> Krishna put it simply: your right is to action — never its fruits.
> Master the work. Results follow.

### POINT 2: Mind (0:24–0:41)
> Lesson two: your mind is your **best friend…**
> …or your worst enemy. Every scroll, every "I'll start tomorrow" — is a choice.
> Train your focus like a muscle.
> The Gita says: lift yourself by yourself. Never let yourself sink.
> Nobody's coming to do it for you.

### POINT 3: Path (0:41–0:58)
> Lesson three: walk **your own path.**
> Stop copying lives you only see in highlight reels.
> Comparison quietly kills your momentum.
> Better your own duty done imperfectly than another's done perfectly.
> Your path, done badly, still beats theirs.

### OUTRO + CTA (0:58–1:08)
> So be honest: are you working for the result… or for the work itself?
> Tell me below. Save this — and follow for more ancient wisdom, made modern.
