Steps of the Ceremony · विवाह के चरण

Tap a step to read its meaning · अर्थ पढ़ने के लिए क्लिक करें

  1. The ceremony begins by worshipping Lord Ganesha, the remover of obstacles, so that the marriage proceeds smoothly. The bride separately worships Goddess Gauri (Parvati), seeking a marriage as devoted as Shiva and Parvati's.
  2. The groom arrives with the baraat and is welcomed by the bride's family with aarti and tilak. Elders of both families embrace — the union of two families, not just two people.
  3. The groom is received at the mandap like an honoured guest and offered madhuparka — a mixture of honey, curd and ghee — symbolising sweetness and purity in the new life.
  4. Bride and groom exchange flower garlands, expressing their free and joyful acceptance of each other.
  5. The bride's parents place her hand in the groom's, entrusting their daughter to him. The groom promises to support her in Dharma (righteousness), Artha (prosperity) and Kama (love) — “dharme cha arthe cha kame cha naticharami”.
  6. The ends of the couple's garments are tied in a sacred knot, symbolising a lifelong, unbreakable bond.
  7. The groom holds the bride's hand and recites the Rigvedic mantra: “I take your hand for good fortune, that you may grow old with me.”
  8. The sacred fire, Agni, is lit as the eternal witness of the marriage. Offerings of ghee and samagri are made with Vedic mantras.
  9. The bride's brother pours puffed rice into her hands, which the couple offer into the fire for prosperity. The couple then circle the fire — the pheras — each round dedicated to a goal of life.
  10. The heart of the Vedic wedding: seven steps taken together, each with a vow. After the seventh step they are friends for life — “sakha saptapadi bhava”. (See the seven vows below.)
  11. The groom applies sindoor to the bride's hair parting and ties the mangalsutra — symbols of their marriage and his commitment to her well-being.
  12. The couple are shown the Pole Star (Dhruva) and Arundhati — symbols of constancy and devotion. Elders and guests shower them with blessings and flowers.

Saptapadi — The Seven Sacred Steps · सप्तपदी के सात वचन

१

Ishe Ekapadi

For nourishment — may we provide for each other and our home.

२

Urje Dvipadi

For strength — physical, mental and spiritual, together.

३

Rayas Poshaya Tripadi

For prosperity — may we earn and share wealth righteously.

४

Mayo Bhavaya Chatushpadi

For happiness — love, respect and harmony with our families.

५

Prajabhyah Panchapadi

For progeny — may we be blessed with noble children.

६

Ritubhyah Shatpadi

For the seasons — a long life together through all of life's seasons.

७

Sakhe Saptapadi

For friendship — may we remain lifelong companions.
