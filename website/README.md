# नियति · Niyati — Hindu Religious Work Services

Website for Aacharya Varadbhai Bhogayata: Hindu rituals, the 16 Sanskaras and Vedic weddings with English explanation.
Static site (HTML/CSS/JS, no build step). Open `index.html` in a browser.

## Adding photos (फ़ोटो कैसे जोड़ें)
Put the photos in the `images/` folder with these names. The site picks them up automatically; until then it shows the built-in artwork.
- `images/acharya.jpg` — photo of Aacharya ji (portrait, about 3:4)
- `images/gallery1.jpg` … `images/gallery6.jpg` — ceremony photos (Vivah, Lakshmi Puja, Havan, Griha Pravesh, Satyanarayan Katha, Saptapadi)

## Other things to update
- Testimonials in "What Families Say" are samples. Replace them with real reviews.
- WhatsApp number: `WHATSAPP_NUMBER` in `js/main.js` and the `wa.me` links in index.html (currently UK +44 7741 922710).

## Design
- Fonts (Google Fonts): Yatra One (नियति wordmark), Cinzel (English caps), Cormorant Garamond (headings), Poppins (body), Tiro Devanagari Hindi.
- Colours: maroon, saffron, marigold and gold.
- Artwork in `images/*.svg` (mandala, diya, mandap, marigold toran, logo) was drawn for this site and is free to use.
