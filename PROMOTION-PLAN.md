# Niyati — Website Launch & Promotion Plan

**Goal:** when someone searches for a *Hindu wedding priest in the UK*, a *Vedic wedding in English* or a *Hindu priest for a destination wedding*, Niyati should appear on page 1 of Google and be the easiest priest to contact.

> No one can guarantee the #1 spot on Google. But a niche like "Vedic wedding **in English**" + "destination" + a real local presence is very winnable. Expect first results in 4–8 weeks and strong positions in 3–6 months if the steps below are followed.

---

## Step 0 — Go live (10 minutes, do today)

1. **Turn on GitHub Pages:** GitHub → repo `Ai-videos-` → **Settings → Pages** → *Source: Deploy from a branch* → Branch **`gh-pages`**, folder **`/ (root)`** → **Save**.
   Within 1–2 minutes the site is live at **https://hinalbhogayata-create.github.io/Ai-videos-/**
2. **Buy your own domain (strongly recommended, ~£10/year).** A name like `niyatiweddings.co.uk`, `niyati.org.uk` or `niyatipriest.com` looks professional and ranks better than a github.io address.
   - Buy it at Namecheap, Cloudflare or GoDaddy.
   - GitHub → Settings → Pages → *Custom domain* → enter it and tick **Enforce HTTPS**.
   - Tell Claude the domain: the canonical link, sitemap and robots.txt then need updating to the new address (a 2-minute change).
3. **Get permissions before promoting.** Get written OK from the couples and families shown in the wedding photos and videos. Also get it from the photographers whose watermarks appear (SDX Visuals, Aravind Rajan, dbmemories).

## Step 3 — Reviews (the biggest ranking factor for local search)

- After every ceremony, send families a WhatsApp message with your **Google review link** (Business Profile → "Ask for reviews"). Also print it as a QR code on a small card.
- Target: **10 reviews in month 1, 30+ in 3 months.** Reply to every review.
- Ask happy couples whether you can quote them on the website, then add a real "What Families Say" section. The sample reviews were removed before launch on purpose.
- Ask the reviewer to mention the city and ceremony, e.g. "Vedic wedding in Edinburgh, explained in English". That helps rankings.

## Step 4 — Get listed where couples look (backlinks)

**Wedding directories** (free or low-cost profiles linking to your site):
- UK: **Hitched.co.uk**, **Bridebook**, **Guides for Brides**, **WeddingWire UK**, Asian-wedding directories (Asian Wedding Magazine, Asiana Wedding, Wedding Affair)
- International: The Knot / WeddingWire (US couples planning in Europe), Zola, WedMeGood (India)
- General: Yell, Thomson Local, Bing Places, Facebook Page

**Partnerships** (each one is a referral source and a backlink):
- **Venues** that host Indian weddings: castles, country houses, hotels. Offer them a one-page "Hindu ceremony at your venue" PDF covering fire-safe havan and timings.
- **Wedding planners** and **mandap / décor companies**: offer a referral thank-you.
- **Photographers & videographers:** ask them to tag Niyati and link your site in their blog posts. In return, credit them on your gallery.
- **Temples & community groups** (e.g. the mandirs you already serve): ask for a link on their "priests / services" page.
- **Destination planners** in Italy, Portugal, Spain, Dubai and Bali who serve Indian couples.
